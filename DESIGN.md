# Design direction

## Clinical Chartbook

SmartHealthCare uses the visual language of a clear, contemporary health reference: warm paper, measured editorial typography, quiet teal accents and fine rules that help people scan. The page should feel calm and useful to someone exploring general health information.

The home page acts as an index. It introduces the tools in plain language, states their experimental and educational limits before a visitor enters, and keeps secondary references available without competing with the main choices. Tool pages retain their task flows and use the shared visual system to make controls, headings and supporting information feel related.

## Tokens

- Background: warm off-white `#f7f6f2`; raised surface: `#fffefa`.
- Text: deep blue-gray `#1d3037`; secondary text: `#53646a`.
- Accent: muted teal `#2e6874`; caution: `#9a5148`.
- Display face: Newsreader. Interface and body face: IBM Plex Sans.
- Controls use clear borders, modest corner rounding and visible keyboard focus. Avoid gradients, decorative shadows, floating overlays and ornamental motion.
- Dark appearance uses charcoal surfaces, soft off-white text and a lighter teal accent.

## Content rules

- Describe model output as an estimate, not a diagnosis.
- Keep experimental and educational limits visible.
- Do not imply clinical validation, accuracy, or treatment guidance without evidence.
- Keep each tool's next action clear and its result easy to scan.
